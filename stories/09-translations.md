# Translated storefront and admin text

As a shopper or administrator using another language, I want the module's labels in my own language, so that the checkout and configuration are easy to understand.

## Acceptance criteria
- All storefront and admin strings are translatable: "Checkout with", "Order faster", "with iDEAL fast checkout", "Create an iDEAL profile and save your address for your next order", "Fast Checkout", "Enter my own address and pay", "PAY. options", "Enable iDEAL Fast Checkout" and its tooltip.
- Translation files are in `i18n/` for 51 locales, such as `nl_NL` ("Checkout with" becomes "Afrekenen via").
